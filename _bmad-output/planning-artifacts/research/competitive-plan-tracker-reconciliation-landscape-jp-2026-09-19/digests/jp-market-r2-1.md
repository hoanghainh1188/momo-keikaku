## Answers to the owned questions
**Q1 Project Online fallout.** Retirement is 2026-09-30 with no read-only grace period (#1). Microsoft steers customers to Planner premium, Project Server SE, or D365 Project Operations (#1). SRI, a Japanese MS Project specialist, markets 5 paths, including its own hosted "Project+" that keeps MS Project desktop compatibility through 2031. Pricing is by quotation only (#2, #3). No Japanese vendor seen offers tracker-ticket (Jira/Backlog) reconciliation as part of migration. The pitch is continuity of the MS Project/WBS experience. Timing: displaced Project Online users are choosing tools right now.

**Q2 TeamSpirit Jira connector.** It is a Ricksoft-built Jira app, free per snippet, launched 2023-01 (stale). It pushes Jira-entered work hours into TeamSpirit 工数実績. Mapping is a default TeamSpirit ジョブ + 作業分類 per Jira project, which is coarse and not per ticket to a WBS task (#4). The official TS Connect 工数 list has no Backlog connector (#5). Any "TeamSpirit integrates with Backlog" claim came only from an aggregator snippet and is unverified. Pricing of the connector: free per snippets, not confirmed on the Marketplace page.

**Q3 Backlog popularity, second source.** Nulab official: over 15,000 paid contracts (Sep 2025), about 1.43M users (#8). The independent BOXIL 1,825-person share survey puts クラウドログ #1 (#12, low confidence). This suggests 工数/予実-focused tools rank well among PM-tool respondents in Japan. No MM総研 or similar share figure was found.

**Q4 Backlog 予実 gaps.** Backlog has one cumulative `estimatedHours` and `actualHours` per issue. Actual hours require Standard plan or above (#9). The API exposes only issue-level fields, with no worklog endpoint and so no who/when breakdown (#10). No Gantt baseline was found. Users build spreadsheet and extension workarounds (#11). The 2027 plan change pushes unlimited-user teams from ¥16k to ¥36.3k/mo and causes public backlash (#6, #7). This creates a price-sensitivity window, and Nulab is adding cross-project Gantt and 孫課題 only on the higher tiers.

**Q5 Vietnam offshore.** The only evidence is job postings listing Redmine, Jira and Backlog (plus Odoo) together as BrSE requirements (#13). No survey or company blog quantifying which tracker dominates was found. It is a mixed-tool environment, which supports a multi-tracker connector strategy.

## Reconciliation-wedge verdict for this cluster
**No.** Nothing retrieved here quantifies tracker work that is unmapped to a plan in hours or cost, or keeps separate baseline and actual ledgers fed by tracker data.
- **TeamSpirit×Jira (partial, adjacent):** feeds tracker hours into a 工数 ledger, but maps at project to job level. It does not flag tickets that belong to no plan task (#4).
- **Backlog:** keeps estimate and actual on the same issue with no baseline and no worklog history (#9, #10). Reconciliation is DIY in spreadsheets (#11).
- **Project+ / Planner migration paths:** WBS and Gantt continuity only (#2, #3).
